Estimation & BOQ

Price the job before you quote it

Each BOQ line holds material, labour, equipment, subcontract and other cost. Pick a product and its cost fills in. Set the margin and the selling price follows.

PRICING RULE
Selling = Unit cost × (1 + Margin %)
Default margin 30%. Approved BOQ lines become the quotation.

Questions

01. Is it really free?
Yes. Fire Alarm Fire Fighting is free to download and use.
02. Which apps does it need?
CRM, Sales, Project, Purchase, Inventory, Invoicing, Employees and Re-Sequences (free). Odoo installs them with FAFF.
03. Can I try it before using real data?
Yes. Configuration → Load Demo Data creates 9 sample jobs, one at each stage from CRM to paid. Clean Demo Data removes them.
04. Who can override the rules?
A FAFF Manager can confirm completion with open rectification and close a job before full payment. FAFF Users follow the normal flow.
05. Can I start from an existing CRM opportunity?
Yes. Press Start FAFF Process on the opportunity and the wizard opens for it.
