Progress Status

Where is this job? One button tells you.

Press Progress Status on an opportunity, estimate, sales order, MO, purchase order, transfer or quality check. A popup shows the overall completion and all 16 steps. The current step and the next one open with their checklist; the rest stay folded.

Checklist items include: estimate approved, quotation sent, sales order confirmed, documents sent and received, MO released, quality passed, GRN received, delivery completed, invoice posted and payment voucher.

Installing switches the web UI to Switchgear mode: the Switchgear sidebar replaces the top submenus on its screens.

The CRM pipeline opens in list view, and Mark Won is hidden on opportunities so jobs move through the cycle.

Configuration and the demo data loader are for administrators only. All internal users get the Switchgear app.

Questions

Q1 Is it only for switchgear?
It is built for switchgear and electrical panel makers, but any make-to-order workshop with estimate, BOM, MO and QC can use it. The demo loader also has a Furniture profile.
Q2 Can I skip the estimate?
Yes. Tick "Use quick quotation (bypass job estimate)" on the opportunity and go straight to the quotation.
Q3 Does Create PR raise a requisition or an RFQ?
It creates a purchase RFQ linked to the MO, with the vendor and lines you confirm in the popup. For an approval step, raise a Purchase Requisition from the sidebar; once approved it creates one RFQ per vendor.
Q4 Where do I start?
Open the Switchgear app, run the Tutorial Wizard once, then follow the processing cycle for real jobs.
